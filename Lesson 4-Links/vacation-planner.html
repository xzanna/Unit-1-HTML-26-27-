My Dream Trip to Singapore

Trip Research

Singapore is a beautiful island city-state in Southeast Asia. Singapore is known for its futuristic landmarks, world-class food scene, and impeccable cleanliness. Go to the official Singapore tourism site to find information on top attractions, events, and travel tips. For reviews and recommendations from other travelers, check out the TripAdvisor page. To plan your visit according to the weather, visit Singapore's Daily Forecast for up-to-date forecasts and climate information.

Flight & Hotel Booking

Transportation Info

When I arrive in Singapore, I will use the Mass Rapid Transit website to take the train. You can also use Google Maps to plan your routes and find directions.
