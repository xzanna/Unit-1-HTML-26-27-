Brownie Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Water
  • Powdered Sugar
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Oil
  • Vanilla Extract

Steps

  1. Mix together wet and dry ingredients first in separate bowls, then combine them together.
  2. Pour the batter into a baking pan lined with parchment paper.
  3. Bake at 325 degrees for 40 to 45 minutes.
  4. Wait for it to cool then enjoy!

Why I chose this recipe.

I chose this recipe because I love to eat brownies and have been making them at a very young age.
