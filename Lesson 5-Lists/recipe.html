Snickerdoodle Cookies Recipe

I picked this recipe because it was one of the first recipes I learned to make. It is also so delicious and easy to make!

  • Ingredients
  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of butter
  • 1.5 cups of sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
  • 2.75 cups of flour
  • 1.5 teaspoons of cream of tartar
  • 0.5 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 0.75 teaspoon of salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, mix butter and sugar together until it is light and fluffy. Then add the eggs and vanilla.
  3. Stir in flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt
  4. In a small bowl, stir together sugar and cinnamon.
  5. Wrap the dough and let it refrigerate for 20-30 minutes. Roll into small balls until round and smooth. Drop into the cinnamon-sugar mixture and coat well.
  6. Place the dough on a parchment paper-lined baking sheet. Bake for 9-11 minutes. Let it cool for several minutes on baking sheet before removing from the pan.
